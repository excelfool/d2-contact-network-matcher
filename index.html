Every contact in a LinkedIn export is a 1st-degree connection — the group you can message directly, so this is your highest-value tier. For 2nd-degree at companies you can't reach yet, scroll to Stage 4 below, which turns these results into a coverage map and one-click LinkedIn searches.
How matching works
  • Both your contact's company and each company on your list are normalized — lowercased, and boilerplate like "Life Insurance Company", "Corp.", "Inc.", "of New York", "The", and punctuation are stripped.
  • A token-overlap + sequence-similarity score (0–100%) is computed against every company on your list; the best match above your threshold wins.
  • Raise strictness to cut false positives (e.g. a generic "National Life" matching the wrong firm); lower it to catch heavily abbreviated entries.
